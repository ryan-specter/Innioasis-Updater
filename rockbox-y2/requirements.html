Rockbox-Y2 · Innioasis Y2

What you need before installing Rockbox-Y2

Almost every failed Rockbox-Y2 install traces back to one of five things: the wrong model, a charge-only cable, a flat battery, a missing driver, or connecting the player before the app was listening. Check them before you start.

Get it in Updater CEFull written tutorial

Hardware and computer

  • An Innioasis Y2 — check the model on the player itself, not on the box.
  • A USB data cable; charge-only cables enumerate nothing.
  • A charged player, and a computer that will not sleep mid-flash.
  • Windows: the MediaTek preloader driver. Linux: USB permissions for 0e8d:*.
  • About a gigabyte of free space for the download and extraction.

The driver and permission details live on the drivers page, and connection-timing failures are covered in troubleshooting.

Which build of Rockbox-Y2

The Y2 port is its own beta line. Read the release notes for the build you are installing before you rely on a theme or on a feature that appeared in a later beta.

  • Y2: only builds listed for Y2; the Y1 package is a different ROM.
  • A stable build for everyday use; nightlies for testing.

Questions people ask

Does the player need to be charged first?

Yes — a flash that loses power mid-write is the one failure worth avoiding.

Does it work on ARM Macs?

Yes. Updater CE ships for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, and the portable ZIP has a macOS package.

Do I need internet on the player?

No. The download happens on the computer.
