Rockbox-Y2 · Innioasis Y2

Undo Rockbox-Y2

Going back is the same route in reverse: choose Y2, choose <strong>Original Software</strong>, pick a stable release, and flash it.

Get it in Updater CEFull written tutorial

Restore the original Y2 software

  1. Open Updater CE and set Device Model to Y2.
  2. Choose Original Software in Software.
  3. Pick a stable release — not a Rockbox one.
  4. Run Install / Restore, then power off and connect at the prompt.
  5. Leave the player alone until the success message, then boot it.

The dedicated route with screenshots is the Y2 restore guide.

Before you restore

  • Back up anything on the card you care about; the stock software does not read .rockbox.
  • Do not delete Rockbox files first — flashing the stock ROM is the supported path.
  • If the flash fails after detection, stop and read troubleshooting before trying again.

Questions people ask

Does restoring wipe my music?

No. The card is not reformatted by the ROM flash; your music files stay where they are.

Will I lose the ability to reinstall Rockbox?

No. Rockbox remains available in the catalogue for this model.

Which Original Software release do I pick?

A stable one listed for the same model. Choosing the other model's stock package is the one mistake worth double-checking.
