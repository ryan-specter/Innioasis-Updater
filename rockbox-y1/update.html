Rockbox-Y1 · Innioasis Y1

Update Rockbox-Y1

Updating Rockbox-Y1 is the same flow as installing it: model, software, release, then the connection prompt. Settings and themes on the card normally survive, because they are not part of the ROM.

Get it in Updater CEFull written tutorial

Update to a newer build

  1. Set Device Model to Y1.
  2. Choose Rockbox, then the newer stable (or the nightly you want).
  3. Run Install / Restore and connect the powered-off player at the prompt.
  4. Boot once, then check the theme and database still look right.

If you keep a backup of .rockbox from before the update, a theme that stops working can be rolled back without losing the whole install.

Stable or nightly?

Stable builds are what most people want: they are the ones the guides and theme notes assume. Nightlies exist to test a specific fix and are marked as previews in the app and on the release pages. The full history is on the Rockbox-Y1 release notes.

Questions people ask

Do I need to uninstall first?

No. The flash writes over the old build.

Will my themes break?

Themes that match the build's resolution keep working; a theme built for the other resolution will not look right.

Can I downgrade?

Yes — the old tags stay published on the mirror, and reflashing an older build is the same operation.
