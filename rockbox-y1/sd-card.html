Rockbox-Y1 and the MicroSD card

Rockbox-Y1 keeps nearly everything you can change on the MicroSD card, inside a folder called <code>.rockbox</code>. That one fact explains most of the questions about settings, themes and resets.

The .rockbox folder

Settings, themes, playlists, caches and the database live there. The firmware itself is on the player's internal flash, so deleting the folder does not uninstall anything — it returns the software to a clean default state and the folder is rebuilt on the next boot.

  1. Power off and remove the card, or connect the player as a USB drive.
  2. Turn on hidden files (macOS: Cmd+Shift+.; Windows: View → Hidden items).
  3. Rename .rockbox to .rockbox-old if you might want it back.
  4. Boot the player again and let it recreate the folder.

Card housekeeping

  • Keep music in ordinary folders; the database sorts by tags, not by directory name.
  • Large libraries: update the database after copying, not during playback.
  • A card that drops out mid-playback is usually a card problem, not firmware.

Questions people ask

Will deleting .rockbox delete my music?

No. Music lives outside that folder; only settings, themes and caches are inside it.

Why can't I see the folder?

Names starting with a dot are hidden by default on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Does a bigger card make the player faster?

No, but a faster card makes the database build and scrolling smoother.
