Install your choice with Updater CE

  1. Open the guide and install Innioasis Updater CE for your computer if you do not have it yet.
  2. In Updater CE, select the model printed on your player from Device Model.
  3. From Software, select the software you want to run on that model.
  4. Select a release, click Install / Restore, then power off and connect the player only when Updater CE asks.

Need the community installer? Open the guide and expand the Updater CE download section.

A note on the two tools

Which tool should I use?

Community creators recommend Innioasis Updater 2.5.1 (Community Edition, “Updater CE”) as the best way to update and customise your player. Built alongside the makers of custom software for Innioasis and Timmkoo devices, Updater CE updates your device without wiping your media or settings—safely keeping your Bluetooth pairings, sound preferences, and onboard music and videos intact. (In contrast, the manufacturer's manual flashing tool wipes internal storage completely, requiring you to recopy media and re-pair Bluetooth devices; microSD cards are not affected by either tool.)

Updater CE also alerts you when new updates become available for your player, guides you step-by-step on screen, and runs reliably across Windows, Linux, and all Macs (Intel and Apple Silicon alike). The manufacturer's Universal Flash Tool (also published as the Shared Flash Tool) and SP Flash Tool still exist for factory recovery, but both are manual, wipe internal storage, and are not needed for a normal install or update. The Universal Flashing Tool is restricted on Mac to Apple Silicon on macOS 15+, lacks Linux support, and offers no update notifications.

Most community walkthroughs, including Catsteal3r's TikTok and Dammit Jeff's “How To Actually Quit Spotify” video, recommend using Updater CE.
