How to Update or Restore Your Innioasis G5

Follow these steps to update or reinstall factory firmware on your G5:

  1. Download Innioasis Updater CE: Install the community tool for your computer (macOS, Windows, or Linux).
  2. Select Model G5: Choose G5 in the Device Model dropdown.
  3. Select Original Software: Updater CE retrieves the official rom_g5.zip package from the Firmware Archive.
  4. Connect and Restore: Put the player into recovery mode or power it off completely as guided by the on-screen instructions, then attach your USB cable. Flashing proceeds automatically.
