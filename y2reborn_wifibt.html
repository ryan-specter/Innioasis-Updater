Setting up Wi-Fi, Bluetooth & FM Radio on Y2Reborn

After flashing Reborn (Y2Linux / Y2Reborn) onto your Innioasis Y2 with Updater CE, follow this quick one-time setup to activate your Wi-Fi, Bluetooth headphone pairing, and FM radio using system.img.

Download system.img (860 MB) Install Guide Why Y2Reborn?

Why does Reborn need system.img?

The MediaTek processor in the Innioasis Y2 uses five proprietary firmware modules for wireless hardware: the Wi-Fi RAM microcode, two Bluetooth patch files, the radio calibration modem image, and chip configuration defaults.

Because these files are proprietary MediaTek software, open-source Y2Linux release images cannot distribute them directly. Instead, they are extracted straight from the official Innioasis Original Software image (system.img, v3.2.0 FM). You only copy this file to the player once: Reborn validates the SHA-256 hashes, extracts the small wireless modules into its permanent data storage, and deletes the large file to leave all your storage free for music.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

  1. Step 1: Download system.img

    Download the official verified Original Software system.img file to your computer. This file is from the v3.2.0 FM package that Reborn expects.

    Download system.img Mirror copy also available on the community Ko-fi blog. File size is approximately 860 MB unpacked.
  2. Step 2: First boot & storage initialization

    Unplug the Y2 from your computer after flashing with Updater CE and turn it on with the Power button. On its first boot, the player will display Setting up storage for a few moments as it converts the storage partition into a 5.4 GB ext4 Internal Storage partition for your music library.

    Once the player boots into the Reborn home screen, restart the player once (hold Power to power off, then press Power again to turn it back on). This one-time restart ensures Internal Storage is granted its full usable disk space.

  3. Step 3: Connect to your computer via USB (MTP)

    Connect your powered-on Y2 to your computer using a USB data cable. The player connects using MTP (Media Transfer Protocol) and appears as Internal Storage (along with your microSD card if inserted).

    Important note for macOS users:

    macOS does not natively support MTP file transfers in Finder. To browse and copy files to your Y2's Internal Storage partition on a Mac, you will need a free MTP application such as OpenMTP (recommended open-source tool) or Android File Transfer. Once open, the player's internal storage folders will show up immediately.

    On Windows and Linux, the player mounts automatically in File Explorer or your file manager without any extra software needed.

  4. Step 4: Copy system.img into the Firmware folder

    Open Internal Storage on the player. Look for a folder named Firmware. If the folder does not exist yet, simply create a new folder named Firmware in the root of Internal Storage.

    Copy (do not move) your downloaded system.img file into that Firmware folder. (You can also copy it into a folder named Firmware on your microSD card if preferred).

  5. Step 5: Automatic radio firmware activation

    Wait approximately 10 to 20 seconds. The Y2 detects system.img automatically, reads the five MediaTek radio firmware modules, verifies each one against its cryptographic SHA-256 hash, and saves them to permanent system storage.

    Once complete, Reborn displays Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are ready! The temporary system.img file and the Firmware folder are automatically deleted from your player to restore your storage space.

  6. Step 6: Connect to Wi-Fi & pair Bluetooth headphones
    • Wi-Fi: Go to Settings → System → Network → Wi-Fi, choose your 2.4 GHz wireless network SSID, enter your password using the clickwheel keyboard, and confirm.
    • Bluetooth: Put your Bluetooth headphones or earbuds into pairing mode. On the Y2, go to Settings → Bluetooth, press the center button to scan, select your headphones, and press connect.
    • FM Radio: Plug in wired 3.5mm headphones to act as an antenna and open FM Radio.
  7. Step 7: Automatic over-the-air (OTA) updates

    From this point onward, every future Y2Linux / Reborn update downloads and installs directly over Wi-Fi without needing a computer: simply navigate to Settings → System → Software Update → Check for Updates → Update Now. Your radio firmware is safely preserved across every update!
