Rondo for Innioasis Y1

A minimalist clickwheel music player firmware designed specifically for the Innioasis Y1 capacitive hardware, focused entirely on responsive playback and clean navigation.

Installation Steps

  1. Launch Innioasis Updater CE on your Windows, Mac, or Linux computer.
  2. Select Y1 from the Device Model dropdown.
  3. Select Rondo from the Software dropdown.
  4. Click Install / Restore and wait for the tool to download and prepare the files.
  5. When prompted by Updater CE, power off your Y1 and connect the USB cable.
  6. Complete post-install setup
