Where the money goes

Running Updater CE, the Themes Gallery and the firmware archive costs about $200 a month. On top of that comes the Apple Developer fee each year, a one-off Mac mini for testing, and the hardware: a player for every model and release worth testing, and some of those players do not survive the work. Community contributions currently cover roughly 15% of the real total; the rest comes out of my own pocket.
